History of Emanuel

Learn about my background and family history

From the Start...

Around 1980-1990, my dad met and married my mom. My dad was born in Ethiopia but has an Eritrean background. My mom was born in Eritrea and also has an Eritrean background. In 2002, my dad left Eritrea for the United States of America because he used to work in the United Nations Mission to Eritrea. Other than working, my dad spent years discussing with the United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement and the Department of Homeland Security to get citizenship in the United States. It was rough, but eventually in 2005 he got asylum in the United States. In 2007, he got his citizenship, including my family in the United States. The rest of my family moved from Eritrea to the US in 2009.

My Journey

On October 25, 2011, I was born in New Jersey, United States of America. I have lived in New Jersey for all my life. I went to a couple of nearby schools in NJ until I got into Forest Street School in 2019 for 2nd Grade. I attended Forest Street School (FSS/FSCS) until 7th Grade in 2025. I then, with the help of my oldest sister, signed up for a scholarship program to Shawnigan Lake School in Canada. When I learned that it could be possible, I was shocked and excited. I then, in 2025, got accepted to Shawnigan Lake School in Canada. I am now a freshman student at Shawnigan Lake School in Canada and hopeful to continue my education there.

After some time, forms, governmental documents, and millions of prayers, I got in. The school accepted me and the Government of Canada gave me my visa to go to school in Canada. I was so excited and at the same time scared of going to a new school. But eventually, I got around my fears. I packed up my suitcase, said goodbye to my parents, and headed out to Canada to pursue my high school education. I finished my first year there at 8th Grade and am now moving to 9th Grade. I'm proud of going to new places and continuing my dreams.
